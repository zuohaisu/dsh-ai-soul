# Homeostasis is machine-continuity evidence, not an identity verdict

Date: 2026-09-05

AI Soul needs to grow without treating every change as either identity death or unrestricted persona drift. Current main already governs individual mutations and bounds current cognition, but those controls do not by themselves provide one longitudinal check across two Soul State snapshots.

The new homeostasis contract therefore checks only hard repository invariants that are mechanically knowable: stable `soulId` and schema lineage, unchanged Genesis/identity-kernel provenance (`identity.createdAt`, `identity.origin`, `identity.invariants`), unchanged relationship covenants, and bounded current cognition across SELF / OTHER / RELATIONAL / beliefs / WORLD.

Human-facing name is deliberately excluded from the protected kernel. A Soul may be unnamed, named later, or renamed while remaining the same machine identity. Mutable cognitive domains are also allowed to change; homeostasis is not stasis.

This check must not be promoted into a philosophical or phenomenological identity verdict. Passing means that governed growth did not violate the machine-level continuity invariants we currently know how to enforce. It does not prove that a human judge will experience the evolved Soul as continuous, and it does not replace real DSH selective-growth evidence, repeated-session observation, model-switch evidence, or the Haisu Test for Samuel.

Invariant: **growth may change current cognition and presentation, but it must preserve protected lineage and bounded continuity constraints unless a separately governed ontology explicitly changes those constraints.**
