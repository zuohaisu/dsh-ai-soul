# Genesis Begins With Evidence, Not a Persona Template

**Date:** 2026-08-27

The Genesis path should not solve onboarding by manufacturing a complete personality.

A new Soul needs only enough explicit evidence to establish that this particular relationship began: a chosen Soul ID/name, the first-meeting timestamp, the participants who were present, and provenance for that event. Everything else should remain absent until experience and governed evolution justify it.

This creates a symmetry with Exodus:

- **Exodus** reconstructs an already-lived identity from historical evidence.
- **Genesis** starts a new identity from the smallest explicit first-meeting evidence.

Neither path should fill uncertainty with attractive persona prose.

Accordingly, `createSoulFromGenesis()` records the first meeting and a Genesis evolution event, but leaves self-model, user-model, beliefs, relationship state, and covenants empty. A covenant is not an onboarding default; it must be established by actual participants and evidence.
