# Forgetting is governed retirement, not history erasure

A persistent Soul must be able to stop carrying a current claim without pretending the claim never existed.

For mutable cognitive state, the first forgetting primitive is therefore **retirement**: an independently reviewed transition removes one exact current claim from canonical state and model context, while immutable evolution evidence records that a governed retirement occurred and why.

This deliberately separates three concerns:

- **current cognition**: what the Soul may use now; retired claims leave this projection;
- **governance/audit**: proof that a retirement occurred remains attributable;
- **physical data erasure**: a separate privacy/storage concern that may require stronger redaction or deletion semantics and is not implied by retirement.

Retirement is fail-closed. The proposal must identify one exact current value. Zero matches means stale or incorrect intent; multiple matches mean ambiguity. Neither case grants deletion authority.

Generic retirement does not extend to identity, identity invariants, or relationship covenants. Those remain outside the generic mutable-state authority boundary.
