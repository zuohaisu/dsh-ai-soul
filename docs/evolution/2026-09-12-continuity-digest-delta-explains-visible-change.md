# Continuity digest delta explains bounded visible change

Date: 2026-09-12

AI Soul now has two deliberately different continuity controls. Homeostasis protects hard machine invariants, append-only lineage, and current-cognition capacity. The continuity digest is a bounded presentation projection of canonical facts that can influence the next model turn. Passing homeostasis therefore does not answer a different question: what changed in the bounded continuity context the model will actually see?

A continuity-digest delta answers only that narrower question. It compares two valid same-Soul states through the existing `projectContinuityDigest()` contract and reports deterministic `added`, `removed`, and `changed` entries by exact `sourcePath`. It also preserves both `omittedEntryCount` values because a bounded projection can hide canonical changes outside its visible window.

This is intentionally not a comprehensive Soul State diff. A result with no visible changes does not prove that the canonical states are equal when either digest omitted entries. It is also not a homeostasis decision, an identity-continuity verdict, or mutation authority.

Opaque `relationship.state` remains outside this projection. Until relationship semantics are explicitly governed and machine-readable, changing opaque relationship data cannot manufacture a continuity-delta claim.

Invariant: **continuity-digest delta explains how bounded model-visible continuity changed; it does not decide whether the Soul remained the same.**
